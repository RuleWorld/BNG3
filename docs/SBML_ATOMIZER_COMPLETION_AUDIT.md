# SBML Atomizer scope and completion audit

This audit records the representable scope and the remaining evidence boundary for the SBML Atomizer. It separates translation behavior from numerical qualification and preserves a historical parity triage without changing any historical verdict to PASS. It does not claim that all SBML is supported.

## What the implementation represents

The modern importer is a conditional lowering from SBML core into BNGL. Parsing, conversion, structural round-trip, and simulation agreement are separate claims. A syntactically valid BNGL artifact does not establish that the source semantics were preserved, and a structural round-trip is not a biological correctness test.

| SBML surface | Current implementation boundary | Implementation and tests checked | Required evidence before claiming support |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

This is not a claim that every Core construct or every Level/Version combination is supported. | `SBMLParser.parse`, `_parse_xml_model`, `_parse_xml_rules`, `_parse_xml_initial_assignments` in `python/bionetgen/atomizer/modern/parser.py`; `tests/python/test_modern_atomizer.py`, `test_atomizer_empty_math.py`, `tests/validation/test_sbml_expectations.py`. | Valid-source checks plus construct-specific behavior tests and official/independent comparisons. |
| MathML | A defined subset lowers to BNGL expressions. Unsupported operators/functions and values outside the finite BNG3 value domain must refuse conversion. In particular, delay semantics are not represented by a parser-only expression rewrite. | `_mathml_to_formula` in `parser.py`; MathML writer validation in `python/bionetgen/atomizer/modern/writer.py`; `test_modern_atomizer_mathml_diagnostics.py`, `test_atomizer_nonfinite_and_reserved.py`, `test_atomizer_sbml_delay_rateof.py`. | Operator-specific tests; independent execution of the source law. |
| Units and coordinates | Unit definitions, model/species/compartment units, substance amount/concentration coordinates, and conversion factors are parsed and used in selected lowering paths. Dimensional consistency and every SBML unit combination are not thereby qualified. | `_parse_xml_units` and conversion-factor extraction in `parser.py`; `_conversion_factor_for_species` and `_conversion_factor_for_reaction` in `writer.py`; `test_modern_atomizer_units.py`, `test_atomizer_amount_based_rates.py`, `test_atomizer_variable_stoichiometry.py`. | Explicit amount/concentration, compartment-size, conversion-factor, and unit-scale cases checked against libRoadRunner. |

Variable, fractional, or otherwise nonrepresentable stoichiometry is conditional and must not be silently rounded or frozen. | `_parse_xml_reactions`, `_parse_xml_reference`, `_fold_static_stoichiometry` in `parser.py`; `test_atomizer_variable_stoichiometry.py`, `test_atomizer_fractional_stoichiometry_reaction.py`. | Static and dynamic stoichiometry boundary cases plus source-coordinate trajectory comparison. |

| Events | Only event forms the runtime can schedule and apply are representable. A remaining fast reaction without an exact reduction cannot be treated as an ordinary reaction and still called supported. | `lower_fast_reactions` in `python/bionetgen/atomizer/modern/fast_reactions.py`; `test_atomizer_fast_reaction.py`. | Exact reduction tests and independent trajectories; otherwise an explicit refusal. |

Models whose validity or behavior depends on them are not qualified. | Constraint counting/reporting in `SBMLParser._parse_xml_model`; diagnostics in `test_modern_atomizer.py::test_playground_parser_reports_unsupported_packages_events_and_constraints`. | Enforced constraint semantics or explicit unsupported classification. |

| `multi` | A bounded subset of Multi molecular types, complexes, seed patterns, species mappings, aliases, reaction mappings, compartment references, and numeric values is parsed. | `fbc`, `qual`, `spatial`, `arrays`, `distrib`, `dyn` | Package content that adds flux balance, logical transitions, geometry/diffusion, arrays, distributions, or dynamic-agent behavior is not imported as core kinetics. | `layout`, `render`, `groups` and annotation namespaces | Diagram or grouping metadata can be omitted when it does not change the mathematical model. | `req` package | The parser currently treats `req` as benign requirements metadata. The package is intended to identify mathematical changes made by other packages, so its content can affect whether package-expanded semantics have been represented. The current blanket benign handling is not qualified for `listOfChangedMaths` or `changedBy` records. The implementation also records finite-value, non-finite-value, spatial-dimension, and missing-rate limitations. Those are support boundaries only when the source is valid and the conversion refuses instead of substituting a materially different model. A source-invalid case must be validated before scoring conversion refusal.

## Historical failures requiring careful interpretation

The four historical `oscSystem` trajectory rows disagree with pinned BNG2 because the canonical BNG2 implementation explicitly marks network-simulation `TotalRate` as unimplemented (`legacy/perl/Perl2/RxnRule.pm:27-28`); `RateLaw.pm:25-26` defines the modifier as a total reaction rate. The exact source, SHA-256 `a86ce0ba68c0d4ccfda65d5b03bc29ed4ac1694dcc25028f2042a31a8698bb67`, declares two DNA copies and `DNA() -> DNA() + mRNA(loc~NUCLEUS) kTranscription*BooleanFunction() TotalRate` (`models/oscillatory_system/oscSystem.bngl:72,91,105`). For the full-flux law, the independently integrated source ODE gives 180.919243934 at 0.02, matching BNG3's 180.919243929. Multiplying the same flux by two DNA copies gives 361.838487868, matching BNG2's 361.838487736. This is a pinned-oracle capability gap, not evidence for changing BNG3 semantics. The old FAIL statuses remain visible, and an exact current-main replay receipt is still required.

The historical triage also identifies symbolic NET initial amounts that reload as zero in the `test_partial_dynamical_scaling` and `nfkb_illustrating_protocols` pathways. PR #392 fixes the native NetReader/ActionDispatch cause and merged at `55b0ebe80d05ffd49df811b52398325c84764999`. The retained partial-scaling and NFkB protocols were both replayed unchanged using a CLI whose C++ source matches current main; all 51-point CDAT/GDAT contracts passed against pinned BNG2 at unchanged `rtol=1e-6`, `atol=1e-9`, with maximum absolute error about `1e-8`. RAS GDP starts at 60,000 and both NFkB symbolic seeds at 50,000. This is focused current-content-equivalent replay evidence, not a broad hosted parity receipt; historical FAIL rows remain in the ledger. Both are separate from Atomizer lowering. The current-source `hybrid_test_hpp` parser failure on valid BNG2 observable syntax is likewise a parser compatibility defect, not proof of broad Atomizer scope. It remains open.


The 55b official run's stochastic failures are source-law adjudicated but remain official FAIL records. For stochastic/00003, source.xml is a valid linear birth-death process (`X(0)=100`, birth propensity `X`, death propensity `1.1 X`; source SHA-256 `c58a50dd86f981150b533dd9af25c38f9ef3aeeedad2f679400e022add77f50e`). The independent closed-form law gives `E[X_t]=100 exp(-0.1t)` and `Var(X_t)=100(2.1/0.1)exp(-0.1t)(1-exp(-0.1t))`. Its factorial moments obey `d m_k/dt = k(1-1.1)m_k + k(k-1)m_(k-1)`, with `m_k(0)=100 falling_factorial k`; converting through order four gives kurtosis 40.33, 48.73, 53.61, 58.99, and 64.95 at the five failing times t=41,43,44,45,46. Thus the large-sample standard deviation of the official variance statistic `Y=sqrt(n/2)(S²/sigma²-1)` is about 4.43, 4.89, 5.13, 5.38, and 5.65, rather than 1 under the Gaussian approximation. This agrees with a direct source-law ensemble simulation: 1,000 independent ensembles of 40,000 trajectories, generated using the exact linear birth-death transition law, yielded `P(|Y|>5)` from 0.22 to 0.35 over t=41–46; the five observed positive statistics (7.65, 8.57, 8.55, 6.88, 5.76) jointly exceeded their observed thresholds in 22 of 1,000 source-law replicates (Monte Carlo standard error about 0.46 percentage points). This is evidence that the guide's Gaussian-based false-positive calculation does not apply to this non-Gaussian source law; it does not excuse the official criterion or prove the simulator correct. Preserve the exact official FAIL verdict and thresholds, and classify this as a statistical-reference calibration issue pending a distribution-aware criterion. For stochastic/00023, run `39793193013` used 2,180 trajectories and five mean statistics exceeded the official range at the final five points (maximum absolute statistic 4.5319). Its immigration-death law has an exact Poisson marginal, but observations across time on each trajectory are dependent; a binomial tail that treats the 100 time-point tests as independent is not justified. The fixed-budget remeasurement on current main is pending; keep its official FAIL verdict pending that receipt and source-law/joint-error calibration.

## Taxonomy corrections in the generated limitation report

The historical expectation manifest had three unclassified cause/owner labels. The reusable reason classifier and generated manifest taxonomy now distinguish them without changing any stage verdict, stage count, or source-report identity:

- `semantic/00950` is an `<infinity>` MathML value, with `mathml` as its cause.
- `semantic/00951` contains non-finite parameter values that the writer approximates by finite BNGL values and explicitly refuses to claim as supported; it is classified as `mathml:non-finite-parameter-approximation`.
- `stochastic/00028` and `stochastic/00032` convert, round-trip, and pass independent comparison. Their official-reference reason names a scheduled-action phase-boundary mismatch, owned by the harness; this is distinct from the suite's random same-priority event-order limits.

The focused raw-reason regression exercises cause and owner assignment without case IDs. The generated limitation document is regenerated from the same historical measurement identity with the corrected taxonomy. Exact-head measurements are still required before updating any verdict or count. The older labels are preserved in the report identity and audit history, not as current causes.

## Completion criteria

This audit does not declare Atomizer completion. Completion requires a current-head full official suite and curated BioModels campaign with pinned source/native identities; all source models validated before product scoring; every non-pass assigned to exactly one primary cause with linked dependencies; current replays for defects and unresolved failures; explicit refusals for unsupported constructs; and no dynamic package or Core feature silently dropped. Independent SBML execution, structural round-trip, and any BNG2 compatibility comparison must remain separately labeled. Historical results, current queued jobs, and parser-level tests cannot substitute for those receipts.
