# SBML Atomizer scope and completion audit

This audit records the representable scope and the remaining evidence boundary for the SBML Atomizer. It separates translation behavior from numerical qualification and preserves a historical parity triage without changing any historical verdict to PASS. It does not claim that all SBML is supported.

## What the implementation represents

The modern importer is a conditional lowering from SBML core into BNGL. Parsing, conversion, structural round-trip, and simulation agreement are separate claims. A syntactically valid BNGL artifact does not establish that the source semantics were preserved, and a structural round-trip is not a biological correctness test.

| SBML surface | Current implementation boundary | Implementation and tests checked | Required evidence before claiming support |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Core Level and Version | The parser retains Level/Version and uses them for version-dependent rules. This is not a claim that every Core construct or every Level/Version combination is supported. | `SBMLParser.parse`, `_parse_xml_model`, `_parse_xml_rules`, `_parse_xml_initial_assignments` in `python/bionetgen/atomizer/modern/parser.py`; `tests/python/test_modern_atomizer.py`, `test_atomizer_empty_math.py`, `tests/validation/test_sbml_expectations.py`. | Valid-source checks plus construct-specific behavior tests and official/independent comparisons. |
| MathML | A defined subset lowers to BNGL expressions. Unsupported operators/functions and values outside the finite BNG3 value domain must refuse conversion. In particular, delay semantics are not represented by a parser-only expression rewrite. | `_mathml_to_formula` in `parser.py`; MathML writer validation in `python/bionetgen/atomizer/modern/writer.py`; `test_modern_atomizer_mathml_diagnostics.py`, `test_atomizer_nonfinite_and_reserved.py`, `test_atomizer_sbml_delay_rateof.py`. | Operator-specific tests; independent execution of the source law. |
| Units and coordinates | Unit definitions, model/species/compartment units, substance amount/concentration coordinates, and conversion factors are parsed and used in selected lowering paths. Dimensional consistency and every SBML unit combination are not thereby qualified. | `_parse_xml_units` and conversion-factor extraction in `parser.py`; `_conversion_factor_for_species` and `_conversion_factor_for_reaction` in `writer.py`; `test_modern_atomizer_units.py`, `test_atomizer_amount_based_rates.py`, `test_atomizer_variable_stoichiometry.py`. | Explicit amount/concentration, compartment-size, conversion-factor, and unit-scale cases checked against libRoadRunner. |
| Reactions and stoichiometry | Constant representable stoichiometry can lower. Variable, fractional, or otherwise nonrepresentable stoichiometry is conditional and must not be silently rounded or frozen. | `_parse_xml_reactions`, `_parse_xml_reference`, `_fold_static_stoichiometry` in `parser.py`; `test_atomizer_variable_stoichiometry.py`, `test_atomizer_fractional_stoichiometry_reaction.py`. | Static and dynamic stoichiometry boundary cases plus source-coordinate trajectory comparison. |
| Rules and initial assignments | Assignment/rate rules and initial assignments have explicit lowering paths. Algebraic rules are not applied as implicit constraints and are reported as dropped; a model depending on them is not supported. | `_parse_xml_rules`, `_parse_xml_initial_assignments` in `parser.py`; `test_atomizer_assignment_rule_targets.py`, `test_atomizer_compartment_initial_assignment.py`, `test_atomizer_algebraic_rule_cases.py`. | Per-rule-type and dependency-order tests; source trajectory comparison. |
| Events | Only event forms the runtime can schedule and apply are representable. Trigger timing, priorities, delays, trigger-time values, and repeated/state-dependent triggers require separate semantics; unsupported forms must refuse. | `_parse_xml_events` in `parser.py`; event lowering in `writer.py`; `test_modern_atomizer_events.py`, `test_atomizer_event_action_semantics.py`, `test_atomizer_event_lowering_defects.py`. | Trigger, priority, delay, assignment, simultaneous-event, and state-trigger tests against an independent simulator. |
| Fast reactions | The importer attempts a fast-reaction reduction for the subset that can be represented. A remaining fast reaction without an exact reduction cannot be treated as an ordinary reaction and still called supported. | `lower_fast_reactions` in `python/bionetgen/atomizer/modern/fast_reactions.py`; `test_atomizer_fast_reaction.py`. | Exact reduction tests and independent trajectories; otherwise an explicit refusal. |
| Constraints | SBML constraints are parsed for detection but are not enforced during simulation. Models whose validity or behavior depends on them are not qualified. | Constraint counting/reporting in `SBMLParser._parse_xml_model`; diagnostics in `test_modern_atomizer.py::test_playground_parser_reports_unsupported_packages_events_and_constraints`. | Enforced constraint semantics or explicit unsupported classification. |
| `comp` | Local/simple composition can flatten; other composition uses libSBML flattening with abort-on-unflattenable behavior and source-relative external-model resolution. | `multi` | A bounded subset of Multi molecular types, complexes, seed patterns, species mappings, aliases, reaction mappings, compartment references, and numeric values is parsed. This is not blanket Multi support. | `parse_multi_package` in `python/bionetgen/atomizer/modern/multi.py`; `test_modern_atomizer_multi.py`, `test_structural_multi_execution.py`. | Construct-by-construct executable cases and structural plus trajectory checks. |
| `fbc`, `qual`, `spatial`, `arrays`, `distrib`, `dyn` | Package content that adds flux balance, logical transitions, geometry/diffusion, arrays, distributions, or dynamic-agent behavior is not imported as core kinetics. These constructs require explicit refusal when they affect model behavior. | Package namespace accounting in `SBMLParser._parse_xml_model`; `test_atomizer_fbc_guard.py`, `test_modern_atomizer_package_diagnostics.py`, `test_sbml_package_severity.py`, `test_modern_atomizer.py::test_playground_parser_reports_unsupported_packages_events_and_constraints`. | Package fixtures proving fail-closed behavior, with annotation-only/empty-package controls. |
| `layout`, `render`, `groups` and annotation namespaces | Diagram or grouping metadata can be omitted when it does not change the mathematical model. Annotation-only namespaces are treated as metadata. | Benign-package policy in `SBMLParser._parse_xml_model`; `test_sbml_package_severity.py`, `test_modern_atomizer_package_diagnostics.py`. | Verify mathematical model preservation and avoid claiming package round-trip preservation. |
| `req` package | The parser currently treats `req` as benign requirements metadata. The package is intended to identify mathematical changes made by other packages, so its content can affect whether package-expanded semantics have been represented. The current blanket benign handling is not qualified for `listOfChangedMaths` or `changedBy` records. Models carrying these constructs are NOT_TESTED unless each referenced package change is resolved. | `benign_packages` handling in `_parse_xml_model` in `parser.py`; generic package tests do not cover `req:listOfChangedMaths` or `req:changedMath`. | Test valid req controls that identify changed math for both supported and unsupported packages; otherwise fail closed or mark the construct unqualified. |
| Unknown packages | Structural elements outside core annotation/notes containers are detected and reported as missing structure; an annotation-only namespace is not treated as a dynamic package. | Namespace/structure scan and package diagnostics in `SBMLParser._parse_xml_model`; `test_sbml_package_severity.py`, `test_modern_atomizer_package_diagnostics.py`. | Required/optional package controls and explicit warning/refusal behavior. |

The implementation also records finite-value, non-finite-value, spatial-dimension, and missing-rate limitations. Those are support boundaries only when the source is valid and the conversion refuses instead of substituting a materially different model. A source-invalid case must be validated before scoring conversion refusal.

## Historical failures requiring careful interpretation

The four historical `oscSystem` trajectory rows disagree with pinned BNG2 because the canonical BNG2 implementation explicitly marks network-simulation `TotalRate` as unimplemented (`legacy/perl/Perl2/RxnRule.pm:27-28`); `RateLaw.pm:25-26` defines the modifier as a total reaction rate. The exact source, SHA-256 `a86ce0ba68c0d4ccfda65d5b03bc29ed4ac1694dcc25028f2042a31a8698bb67`, declares two DNA copies and `DNA() -> DNA() + mRNA(loc~NUCLEUS) kTranscription*BooleanFunction() TotalRate` (`models/oscillatory_system/oscSystem.bngl:72,91,105`). For the full-flux law, the independently integrated source ODE gives 180.919243934 at 0.02, matching BNG3's 180.919243929. Multiplying the same flux by two DNA copies gives 361.838487868, matching BNG2's 361.838487736. This is a pinned-oracle capability gap, not evidence for changing BNG3 semantics. The old FAIL statuses remain visible, and an exact current-main replay receipt is still required.

The historical triage also identifies symbolic NET initial amounts that reload as zero in the `test_partial_dynamical_scaling` and `nfkb_illustrating_protocols` pathways. PR #392 fixes the native NetReader/ActionDispatch cause and merged at `55b0ebe80d05ffd49df811b52398325c84764999`. The retained partial-scaling and NFkB protocols were both replayed unchanged using a CLI whose C++ source matches current main; all 51-point CDAT/GDAT contracts passed against pinned BNG2 at unchanged `rtol=1e-6`, `atol=1e-9`, with maximum absolute error about `1e-8`. RAS GDP starts at 60,000 and both NFkB symbolic seeds at 50,000. This is focused current-content-equivalent replay evidence, not a broad hosted parity receipt; historical FAIL rows remain in the ledger. Both are separate from Atomizer lowering. The current-source `hybrid_test_hpp` parser failure on valid BNG2 observable syntax is likewise a parser compatibility defect, not proof of broad Atomizer scope. It remains open.


The current report's only official failure, stochastic/00003, remains an official FAIL but has a source-law explanation for the statistical mismatch. Its valid SBML source is a linear birth-death process (`X(0)=100`, birth propensity `X`, death propensity `1.1 X`; source SHA-256 `c58a50dd86f981150b533dd9af25c38f9ef3aeeedad2f679400e022add77f50e`). The independent closed-form law gives `E[X_t]=100 exp(-0.1t)` and `Var(X_t)=100(2.1/0.1)exp(-0.1t)(1-exp(-0.1t))`. Its factorial moments obey `d m_k/dt = k(1-1.1)m_k + k(k-1)m_(k-1)`, with `m_k(0)=100 falling_factorial k`; converting through order four gives kurtosis 40.33, 48.73, 53.61, 58.99, and 64.95 at the five failing times t=41,43,44,45,46. Thus the large-sample standard deviation of the official variance statistic `Y=sqrt(n/2)(S²/sigma²-1)` is about 4.43, 4.89, 5.13, 5.38, and 5.65, rather than 1 under the guide's Gaussian approximation. This agrees with a direct source-law ensemble simulation: 1,000 independent ensembles of 40,000 trajectories, generated using the exact linear birth-death transition law, yielded `P(|Y|>5)` from 0.22 to 0.35 over t=41–46; the five observed positive statistics (7.65, 8.57, 8.55, 6.88, 5.76) jointly exceeded their observed thresholds in 22 of 1,000 source-law replicates (Monte Carlo standard error about 0.46 percentage points). This is evidence that the guide's Gaussian-based false-positive calculation does not apply to this non-Gaussian source law; it does not excuse the official criterion or prove the simulator correct. Preserve the exact official FAIL verdict and thresholds, and classify this as a statistical-reference calibration issue pending a distribution-aware criterion. For stochastic/00023, the cf93 run passed at 3,611 trajectories under the then-current adaptive planner. That is not a qualification of the fixed-work-budget planner in PR #386. Its immigration-death law has an exact Poisson marginal, but observations across time on each trajectory are dependent; a binomial tail that treats the 100 time-point tests as independent is not justified. Record the adaptive pass as measured and keep fixed-budget statistical qualification pending.

The labels change only classification, not the scientific results or counts. The report remains a historical measurement when later code changes, including PR #386, are merged.

## Completion criteria

This audit does not declare Atomizer completion. Completion requires a current-head full official suite and curated BioModels campaign with pinned source/native identities; all source models validated before product scoring; every non-pass assigned to exactly one primary cause with linked dependencies; current replays for defects and unresolved failures; explicit refusals for unsupported constructs; and no dynamic package or Core feature silently dropped. Independent SBML execution, structural round-trip, and any BNG2 compatibility comparison must remain separately labeled. Historical results, current queued jobs, and parser-level tests cannot substitute for those receipts.
